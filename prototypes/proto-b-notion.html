The Gauntlight

Abomination Vaults — Chapter 3
🕐 Edited 2 min ago 📄 2,847 words 🔖 Auto-saved
+ ⋮⋮

The crumbling lighthouse known as the Gauntlight has long been a source of dread for the people of Otari. Now, strange lights have begun emanating from its peak once more, and eerie sounds echo from beneath its foundation.

+ ⋮⋮

Part 1: Unlikely Heroes

+ ⋮⋮

The adventure begins when the player characters learn about disturbances near the Gauntlight. Mayor Oseph Menhemes has posted notices seeking adventurers willing to investigate.

+ ⋮⋮

Corpselight

◉◉
Creature 2
Uncommon Undead Incorporeal
Perception +8; darkvision
Languages Common (can't speak any language)
AC 17; Fort +5, Ref +9, Will +8
HP 30; Immunities death effects, disease, paralyzed, poison, precision, unconscious
Speed fly 30 feet
Melee ◉ ghostly touch +10, Damage 1d8+3 negative
Phosphorescent Lure ◉◉ (mental, visual) The corpselight flares with sickly light. All creatures within 30 feet must attempt a DC 18 Will save or become fascinated.
+ ⋮⋮

Encounter: Graveyard Approach

+ ⋮⋮

As the heroes approach the lighthouse grounds, they pass through an overgrown graveyard. Perception DC 18 to notice disturbed graves.

+ ⋮⋮
GM Note

If the players attempt to rest in the graveyard, have 1d4 corpselights emerge from the disturbed graves at midnight. This encounter can be avoided entirely by succeeding at a DC 20 Survival check to find a safe campsite.

+ ⋮⋮
Haunted Graveyard

While within the graveyard, characters take a −1 status penalty to saves against fear effects. Characters with the Undead Lore skill can attempt a DC 16 check to negate this penalty for themselves.

+ ⋮⋮

The path continues north through twisted oak trees before reaching the lighthouse foundation. A heavy iron door — rusted but intact — blocks the entrance.
